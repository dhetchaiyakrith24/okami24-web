article (buyer questions the posters do not answer):
- H3 "G4 Pro or G4 Pro KOKO?" — Both have a touchscreen, 35W and GPP pods. The G4 Pro is the slim one: a 2.51-inch screen, 1800mAh, about 78g. The [G4 Pro KOKO](/products/caliburn-g4-pro-koko) is the box-shaped one: a 2.0-inch screen, 2000mAh, about 93g, and five themes you collect by blind draw.
- H3 "What does the G4 Pro add over the standard G4?" — The [G4](/products/caliburn-g4) has a 0.85-inch colour screen run by one button and two modes. The G4 Pro swaps the button for a touchscreen, adds the Custom mode and a 1800mAh battery against the G4's 1300mAh.
